# Type-Specific Reverse-Prompt Modules

Load only the modules matching the image. Combine modules when the image genuinely combines types.

## Product and e-commerce

Prioritize:

- exact product view, crop, scale, orientation, and hero/detail hierarchy;
- verified geometry, openings, controls, hardware, labels, color, and material truth;
- contact, support, reflections, transparent thickness, highlight width, and surface separation;
- background value, copy safety, channel ratio, and whether text is composited later;
- distinction between visual metaphor and verified function/claim.

Do not infer engineering function from color or shape. A style reference cannot become product truth.

## Lifestyle and campaign photography

Prioritize:

- the real trigger for the action and the before/during/after moment;
- body weight, balance, hand-object relationship, gaze, effort, and whether the action is candid or posed;
- casting, wardrobe silhouette, fabric behavior, grooming, props, and logo avoidance;
- location evidence, weather, life traces, localization, and current-market objects;
- subject/environment perspective and lighting integration.

Mark behaviors or localization assumptions `unknown` until researched or user-approved.

## Landscape and environment

Prioritize:

- terrain/architecture, weather, time cue, season, scale, and accessibility;
- foreground/midground/background layers, atmospheric perspective, horizon, and leading forms;
- sky-to-ground value ratio, light direction, haze density, and color temperature separation;
- whether the image is documentary, idealized, surreal, or synthetic.

Do not confuse dramatic clouds with a verified storm or a time-of-day claim.

## Typography, logo, and graphic poster

Prioritize:

- glyph/letter proportions, stroke behavior, serif logic, spacing, line breaks, alignment, and hierarchy;
- text-to-image area ratio, grid, negative space, border, ornament, and information density;
- flat/embossed/extruded behavior, edge bevel, surface finish, shadow, and print/screen texture;
- whether exact text must be composited rather than generated.

Do not reproduce protected logos or distinctive lettering as a generic style. Preserve layout principles, not identity.

## Illustration

Prioritize:

- shape language, silhouette, line weight, edge control, fill/paint method, texture, and abstraction level;
- palette size and value grouping, not just color names;
- perspective convention, spatial flattening, exaggeration, and mark-making rhythm;
- medium evidence versus speculative artist/tool naming.

Avoid naming a living artist as a style shortcut. Translate visible techniques instead.

## 3D and CGI

Prioritize:

- modeling language, bevel scale, topology-visible clues, stylization, and detail density;
- shader response: roughness, subsurface behavior, transmission, refraction, metallic response, and imperfections;
- key/fill/rim relationships, environment reflections, contact shadows, and render cleanliness;
- camera perspective, depth, and physically plausible assembly.

Treat render engine and software names as inference unless known.

## Character and IP

Prioritize:

- silhouette, head/body ratio, facial construction, expression range, pose, costume layers, and signature accessories;
- material language for toy, plush, resin, illustration, or realistic character;
- front/side/back consistency if downstream production requires it;
- which traits are transferable archetype versus protected identity.

Do not reconstruct a protected character for substitution without a rights-aware boundary.

## Mixed technical or explanatory image

Prioritize:

- what is literal product truth versus diagrammatic metaphor;
- layer order, callout hierarchy, arrows, labels, and whether text should be added in post;
- which deformation, cutaway, transparency, or exploded relationship has verified structural evidence;
- whether the image explains a mechanism or merely evokes one.

If engineering evidence is missing, mark the mechanism `conceptual_visualization` and require human/product review.
